Why You Should Visit Zanzibar

More than just a destination,it's an experience.

From beautiful turquoise beaches🌊 and rich swahili culture to delicious local food and unforgettable sunset,zanzibar has something special waiting around every corner.Whether you're looking for an adventure,relaxation or little escape from every day life,zanzibar is the place to be.

Come explore,discover and make memories that stay with you!😉

Top Attractions in Zanzibar

Discover the hidden gems of Zanzibar.

Prison Island

Explore the beautiful island famous for its giant Aldabra tortoises and historical buildings.

Nungwi Beach

Enjoy the breathtaking beach famous for white sand, swimming and beautiful sunsets.

Rock Restaurant

A unique restaurant built on a rock in the Indian Ocean.

Jozani Forest

Explore the tropical forest famous for the rare Zanzibar red colobus monkeys, forest walks and mangrove swamps.

Mnemba Atoll

Discover Mnemba Atoll, famous for snorkelling, diving and colourful marine life.

Experiences You Will Love

Go beyond sightseeing and experience the magic of Zanzibar.

Sunset Dhow Cruise

Sail along the Indian Ocean aboard a traditional wooden dhow as the sun sets.

Spice Farm Tour

Smell, touch and taste fresh spices while learning about Zanzibar's spice history.

Stone Town Walking Tour

Wander through historic streets, beautiful architecture and traditional markets.

Forodhani Night Market

Taste local street food and experience Zanzibar's lovely evening atmosphere.

Kayaking and Water Sports

Enjoy kayaking, paddleboarding and other activities along the coast.

Other Activities to Enjoy

  • Scuba Diving.
  • Shopping for local souvenirs.
  • Horseback Riding.
  • Kitesurfing.

Things to Carry

  • Sunscreen and sunglasses
  • Light and comfortable clothes
  • Swimwear and beach towels
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Camera and phone charger
  • Insect repellent
  • Passport and travel documents

The Best Time to Visit Zanzibar

The best time to visit Zanzibar is between June and October when the weather is sunny and pleasant. December to February is also a great time to visit because of the warm weather and beautiful ocean conditions. These months are perfect for beach activities, sightseeing and exploring the island.
