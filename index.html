🌴Karibu Zanzibar!

Some places make you smile, but Zanzibar makes you wish you could stay forever.
From its charming streets and rich Swahili heritage to its breathtaking beaches and mouthwatering flavours, every corner has a story to tell.
So slow down, soak it all in and let this little island steal your heart!⛅

Top Attractions

Nungwi Beach

Soft white sands, crystal-clear waters and sunsets that make you forget what time it is. Nungwi is the perfect place to relax, swim and enjoy the island life.

Prison Island

Meet the island's oldest residents! Prison Island is home to giant Aldabra tortoises, some over 100 years old. They're slow walkers, but definitely worth meeting!

The Rock Restaurant

A restaurant sitting on a rock in the middle of the ocean? Yes, really! Enjoy delicious food, beautiful ocean views and an experience you won't find just anywhere.

Jozani Forest

Step into the beautiful Jozani Forest and meet Zanzibar's famous red colobus monkeys. Enjoy the greenery, discover unique wildlife and experience the island's natural beauty.

Top 5 Things To Do

  1. Sail on a sunset dhow cruise
  2. Smell and taste fresh spices on a spice farm tour
  3. Walk through the old streets of Stone Town
  4. Snorkel with colourful fish at Mnemba Atoll
  5. Try street food at Forodhani Night Market

Things To Carry

  • Sunscreen and sunglasses
  • Light, comfortable clothes
  • Swimwear and a beach towel
  • Camera and phone charger
  • Passport and travel documents

Best Time To Visit

The best time to visit is between June and October, when the weather is sunny and cool. December to February is also great, with warm days and calm blue water, perfect for the beach!
